Digital Marketing Executive

Witney Permanent £27,000 - £30,000 per year View Job Description
As a Digital Marketing Executive near Witney, you'll play an essential role in supporting the marketing team within the manufacturing industry majorly by email marketing. This position ensures the smooth execution of marketing activities, helping to enhance the company's digital presence and drive its objectives forward.

About Our Client

My client is a well-established company within the manufacturing industry based near Witney, recognised for its structured processes and efficient operations. As a mid-sized organisation, they are dedicated to maintaining high-quality standards in their field.

Job Description

Key responsibilities of the Digital Marketing Executive include:

    • Create and send email marketing campaigns to customers and distributors around the world.
    • Plan when marketing emails and communications are sent.
    • Work with sales, product, and marketing teams to promote new products and events.
    • Use marketing software to automate emails and customer journeys.
    • Keep customer contact data organised and GDPR compliant.
    • Track results such as email opens, clicks, and leads generated.
    • Test different email versions to see which performs best.
    • Produce reports and suggest ways to improve future campaigns.
    • Help follow up with potential customers after events and exhibitions.



The Successful Applicant

The successful Digital Marketing Executive will have:

  • Previous experience in email marketing (essential).
  • Be data-driven and understand data/CRMs is beneficial.
  • Be able to collaborate with a team efficiently whilst also work independently.
  • Be proactive and hardworking with a positive attitude to work.
  • Full UK driving licence and car required due to rural location of office.

What's on Offer

Benefits of the Digital Marketing Executive include:

  • A competitive salary
  • Bonus structure
  • Great career progression opportunities
  • Full training and mentoring provided
  • A comprehensive benefits package
  • Access to many company perks
  • Generous annual leave package
  • Free parking onsite
